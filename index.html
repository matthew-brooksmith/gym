Knee rules this plan follows

  • No deep squats, lunges, jumps or pivoting. Loaded knee bend stays at or under about 60°, where the kneecap is seated in the groove.
  • Quads are built with closed-chain, guided movements (shallow leg press, low step-ups) plus Spanish squat holds.
  • VMO and stability work every lower day: terminal knee extensions, hip abduction, Copenhagen planks, single-leg balance.
  • Leg size comes mostly from glutes, hamstrings, adductors and calves, which can be loaded hard with the knee still.
  • Stop a set if the kneecap feels like it is shifting. This is general training guidance, so a physio's advice for your knee wins over it.
